
<h1>SharpEdge – CA Firm Management System</h1>

<p>A <strong>Laravel-based management application</strong> developed for a <strong>Chartered Accountancy (CA) firm</strong> to organize client management, service sales, service orders, invoices, payments, document workflows, staff tasks, and accounting operations through role-based panels.</p>

<h3>Key Features &amp; Panel Responsibilities</h3>

<h4>1. Admin Panel</h4>
<ul>
    <li>Manages <strong>client records, service categories, and service orders</strong>.</li>
    <li>Oversees <strong>invoice-related operations, payments, receipts, and payment follow-ups</strong>.</li>
    <li>Handles order management, document verification, and task verification.</li>
    <li>Manages staff workflows, leave approvals, attendance, meetings, and notices.</li>
    <li>Monitors business activities and available operational reports.</li>
</ul>

<h4>2. Marketing Panel</h4>
<ul>
    <li>Coordinates with clients and manages <strong>service purchases and service orders</strong>.</li>
    <li>Tracks client requirements, purchased services, and order progress.</li>
    <li>Handles <strong>invoice tracking, payment records, outstanding dues, and payment follow-ups</strong>.</li>
    <li>Manages client meetings, documents, support tickets, and related activities.</li>
    <li>Uses dashboard statistics to monitor client activity and business operations.</li>
</ul>

<h4>3. Back Office Panel</h4>
<ul>
    <li>Handles <strong>accounting operations and financial computations</strong>.</li>
    <li>Works with <strong>balance sheets, profit and loss statements, trading accounts, and financial reports</strong>.</li>
    <li>Supports reconciliation of assets, debtors, creditors, bank, sales, and purchases.</li>
    <li>Manages assigned tasks, document uploads, and payment follow-ups.</li>
    <li>Supports the processing and review of accounting-related information.</li>
</ul>

<h4>4. Client Panel</h4>
<ul>
    <li>Allows clients to track <strong>purchased services and service-order status</strong>.</li>
    <li>Provides access to available <strong>invoices and payment information</strong>.</li>
    <li>Supports document submissions and client-related requests.</li>
    <li>Allows clients to create and track support tickets.</li>
</ul>

<h3>How the System Works</h3>

<h4>1. Client Registration and Service Purchase</h4>
<p>Client information is maintained in the system. The Marketing team coordinates with clients, manages their service requirements, and records service purchases and related service orders. The Admin Panel oversees client and service records.</p>

<h4>2. Service Order Processing</h4>
<p>Service orders provide a structured record of the services requested or purchased by clients. The relevant teams can track order information and status, helping them organize client requirements and monitor pending work.</p>

<h4>3. Task Management and Back Office Processing</h4>
<p>Administrative task-management workflows support task organization, assignment, and verification. Back Office staff can access their assigned tasks, work on accounting-related activities, manage relevant documents, and track work progress through the available task-management features.</p>

<h4>4. Invoice and Payment Management</h4>
<p>The system supports invoice-related operations and payment tracking. The Admin and Marketing teams can manage relevant payment records, monitor outstanding dues, and perform payment follow-ups. Clients can access available invoice and payment information through their dedicated panel.</p>

<h4>5. Accounting and Financial Reporting</h4>
<p>The Back Office handles financial computations, reconciliation, and accounting reports, including balance sheets, profit and loss statements, trading accounts, and other financial records. These features help organize financial information for review and reporting.</p>

<h4>6. Administration and Client Support</h4>
<p>The Admin Panel oversees operational workflows, while the Marketing and Back Office teams manage their respective responsibilities. Clients can track services, access available billing information, submit documents, and raise support requests through the client panel.</p>

<h3>Business Impact &amp; Workflow Automation</h3>

<p>The platform helps <strong>organize and streamline the CA firm's day-to-day operations</strong> by bringing client records, service orders, payment tracking, task management, document workflows, and financial operations into a centralized application.</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Centralized Client Management:</strong> Keeps client and service-related information organized in one application, making it easier to manage multiple client relationships.</li>
    <li><strong>Structured Service Workflow:</strong> Organizes service purchases and orders so that teams can track client requirements and order progress more systematically.</li>
    <li><strong>Improved Task Coordination:</strong> Supports task assignment, progress tracking, and verification, helping staff organize responsibilities and pending work.</li>
    <li><strong>Invoice and Payment Visibility:</strong> Keeps invoice-related information, payment records, and follow-up activities accessible to the relevant teams.</li>
    <li><strong>Reduced Manual Coordination:</strong> Centralized records and role-based workflows reduce dependence on scattered records and repetitive manual information sharing.</li>
    <li><strong>Better Team Collaboration:</strong> Separate Admin, Marketing, Back Office, and Client panels provide role-appropriate access to information and operational features.</li>
    <li><strong>Organized Financial Operations:</strong> Financial computation, reconciliation, and reporting modules help structure accounting-related work.</li>
    <li><strong>Support for Business Growth:</strong> Standardized workflows help the company manage a growing volume of clients, service orders, and follow-up activities more systematically.</li>
</ul>

<p>Overall, the application supports a more <strong>organized, traceable, and workflow-driven business process</strong>, helping the company coordinate client services, monitor operational activities, and manage financial information from a centralized platform.</p>
